# Vehicle image and rate disclosures

Every vehicle card in this ZIP uses an **included local WebP illustrative image**. The images were generated for this website concept; **they are NOT verified photographs of the listed model, BRACEPLACE-AUTO stock, or a physical rental vehicle**. No third-party photo credits are asserted for the generated assets.

The catalog's `photo` and `photoCredit` data fields are legacy remote photo leads and are **not used for display**. Do not present them as validated working photo links.

Prices are third-party published market examples (some vehicle-model-specific; others **class examples or other trims**) with a source URL, and are not offers, inventory, guaranteed prices or real-time quotes from BRACEPLACE-AUTO. See `data/vehicles.json` for each item's `rateBasis`, `rateLocation` and `source`. Review supplier sites for changes.

Replace generated artwork with authorized, true-to-vehicle photographs and replace market examples with your own verified prices before offering actual rentals.
